# OmniFlow Digital — Claude Instructions

## CRITICAL RULE: No Fake Information

**Do NOT add any fabricated, invented, or placeholder data to this website.**

This applies strictly to:
- Client names, testimonials, or reviews (real client reviews only — none exist yet)
- Statistics (client counts, satisfaction rates, years in business, revenue claims)
- Case studies with made-up results or percentages
- Team member names, bios, or photos
- Pricing (only update if the owner confirms the actual prices)
- Performance claims backed by no real data (e.g. "150 clients served", "98% satisfaction")

If a section requires real data that does not yet exist, leave it as a clearly marked placeholder or honest "coming soon" state — do not fill it with invented content.

## What IS Acceptable

- Service descriptions (what OmniFlow offers)
- Process descriptions (how they work)
- Pricing plans (if confirmed as real by the owner)
- Service promise timelines (e.g. "3–7 day launch" if that is a genuine commitment)
- Geographic focus (Trinidad & Tobago)
- Contact details (WhatsApp, email) — only if confirmed real
